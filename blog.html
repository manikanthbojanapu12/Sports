Coach's Desk Notes

Week 01 — Warm Up Like a Pro

A rushed warm-up is the #1 cause of avoidable soft-tissue injuries we see. We now run a mandatory 12-minute dynamic warm-up before every session — hips, ankles, and shoulders first, ball work last.

Fuel For Performance

Sports Nutrition & Hydration Blueprint

Optimizing macronutrient timing and electrolyte replenishment directly impacts stamina, anaerobic buffering capacity, and muscle protein synthesis.

3 Hours Pre-Match

Complex Glycogen Loading

Slow-digesting low-glycemic carbohydrates (sweet potato, oats, brown rice) with lean protein (25g) to sustain steady blood glucose through 90 minutes.

+500ml Electrolyte Water
During Match / Drills

Intra-Workout Electrolytes

6% carbohydrate-electrolyte fluid providing 30g fast-acting carbs per hour with 400mg sodium and potassium to prevent cramping in hot turf conditions.

150ml Every 15 Mins
30 Mins Post-Match

Anabolic Recovery Window

3:1 ratio of high-glycemic carbohydrates to rapid-absorbing whey protein isolate (30g) + 5g creatine monohydrate to jumpstart muscle repair.
